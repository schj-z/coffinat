Sleep goal

Set the level you want to be under by bedtime. As a rough guide, lower is better for sleep, and caffeine taken within about six hours of bed can measurably disrupt it. There is no established "safe" blood-caffeine level for sleep, so treat this as a personal target you choose — not a recommendation. See sources below.

How this works

Each drink is modelled with a one-compartment pharmacokinetic (Bateman) model: caffeine is absorbed over roughly the first ~45 minutes (so your level rises gradually, it does not jump) and then eliminated with a half-life of about 5 hours. Concentration is estimated in mg/L of blood using a volume of distribution of ≈0.6 L/kg × your body mass. Each day also includes the residual caffeine carried over from the previous days. Constants are population averages from published pharmacology; your own metabolism may differ substantially.

Your privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Your body mass, drinks, times, sleep goal and any custom product names are computed locally and saved only in this browser's localStorage. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere — the app makes no network requests and uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Clearing your browser storage deletes your data; there is no account and nothing on any server. Because the page is public, treat anything you would not want public as such — but your entries never leave your device.

Limitations

This is an educational model, not a measurement. Every constant is a population average; real caffeine metabolism varies widely with genetics, smoking, pregnancy, medication, liver function and more, and the effect bands depend heavily on tolerance. The home-brew figures are rough estimates that ignore grind, agitation and water chemistry. The model shows an estimate; it does not tell you an amount that is safe to drink. Not medical advice — do not use it for medical or dosing decisions.

Sources

All constants are population averages from published sources:

  • Caffeine pharmacokinetics — StatPearls, NIH (NBK519490); Alsabri et al., J. Caffeine Res. 2018; EFSA caffeine assessment.
  • Daily limits (400 mg/day; 200 mg single dose) — EFSA Scientific Opinion on caffeine (2015).
  • Caffeine and sleep timing — Drake et al., J. Clin. Sleep Med. 2013 (caffeine 0, 3 or 6 h before bed all disrupted sleep).
  • Effect / toxicity bands — Caffeine Toxicity, StatPearls (NBK532910) (therapeutic ≈4–8 mg/L, toxicity from ≈15 mg/L, ≈80–100 mg/L potentially lethal).
  • Brew extraction (bean pool, method efficiency) — Arabica vs Robusta & by method; how brew methods affect caffeine.
