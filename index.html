How this works

Each drink is modelled with a one-compartment pharmacokinetic (Bateman) model: caffeine is absorbed over roughly the first ~45 minutes (so your level rises gradually, it does not jump) and then eliminated with a half-life of about 5 hours. Concentration is estimated in mg/L of blood using a volume of distribution of ≈0.6 L/kg × your body mass. Constants are population averages from published pharmacology; your own metabolism may differ substantially.
